Demo Exam Portal

A sample app that sends OTPs and exam announcements through the Eclipse Labs Mail Service. In development every email lands in Mailpit (a dummy inbox); nothing leaves this machine.

1. Log in with a one-time code

2. Announce an exam to many candidates

Bulk mail goes through a rate-capped lane, so send an OTP while the announcement runs: it still arrives within a second or two. With Mailpit, accepted_local (handed to the mail server) is the last status; with the self-hosted Postfix stack, messages continue to accepted_remote.
